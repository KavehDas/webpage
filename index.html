About Me

I am a mathematician specializing in the representation theory and character theory of finite groups. My research focuses particularly on Isaacs π-partial characters, monomial characters, character degrees, and their connections with the structure of finite groups.

I was born in Heris, East Azerbaijan, Iran, in 1995. I earned my PhD in mathematics from Tarbiat Modares University in Tehran under the supervision of Prof. Ali Iranmanesh.

From 2024 to 2025, I held a postdoctoral position at Tarbiat Modares University, where I continued my research in representation theory and finite group theory.

In December 2025, I began a postdoctoral appointment at the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research (BICMR), Peking University, under the supervision of Prof. Jiping Zhang. I am currently undertaking a research stay at RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau, Germany (September 2026–March 2027), hosted by Prof. Gunter Malle.

Research Interests

  • Representation theory of finite groups
  • Ordinary and modular character theory
  • Isaacs π-partial characters of π-separable groups
  • Monomial characters and character degrees
  • Structural properties of finite groups determined by character-theoretic data
